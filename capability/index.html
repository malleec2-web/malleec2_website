What we do

Frontier
Digital Engineering.

Mallee C2 designs, integrates and supports secure digital environments for places where conventional technology architectures are not enough.

We bring together compute, data, AI, applications, communications and device management to deliver capability across connected, disconnected, remote, austere and high-assurance environments.

01

Secure Compute

Modern compute infrastructure designed around the workload, environment and security requirement. From enterprise infrastructure to small-form-factor and deployable systems, we put compute where it is needed.

02

AI

Private and sovereign AI environments designed to bring advanced capability closer to the data and the people using it.

Where required, AI workloads can operate locally without dependence on continuous external connectivity or external AI services.

03

Data & Data Sovereignty

Data sovereignty is about maintaining control over where data resides, where it is processed, how it moves and who has access to it.

Mallee C2 designs secure data environments that enable organisations to collect, store, process and use information within defined sovereign, security and regulatory boundaries. This can include architectures where sensitive data and workloads remain entirely within customer-controlled infrastructure.

04

Edge Device Management

Deploying technology into remote and austere environments creates a different management challenge.

Mallee C2 designs environments that enable distributed devices and systems to be securely configured, monitored, updated and supported across intermittent, constrained and disconnected networks.

From specialist systems to large distributed fleets, management and sustainment are engineered into the architecture from the beginning.

05

Integration

The value isn’t in another standalone technology.

We bring infrastructure, software, data, AI, communications, devices and security together as a complete environment.

Our role is to make the technology work together — and keep it working.

Data where it needs to be.

For many organisations, the question is no longer simply how to store data. It’s where that data can exist, where it can be processed and who ultimately controls it.

Mallee C2 engineers environments that allow sensitive data and workloads to remain within defined geographic, organisational and security boundaries. That can mean keeping data within Australia, within a customer’s own infrastructure, within a specific facility, or even within an individual deployed system.

The same principle extends to AI. Sensitive information can be processed locally without automatically being transferred to an external platform or service.

Sovereignty by architecture – not policy alone.

Small
form factor. Serious
capability.

Digital infrastructure no longer needs to fill a data centre.

Mallee C2 designs compact computing environments capable of supporting sophisticated applications, data processing and AI workloads in locations where traditional infrastructure isn’t practical.

These environments can be fixed, mobile or deployable and integrated with the communications infrastructure available at the location.

Small-form-factor infrastructure can extend sophisticated digital capability into vehicles, vessels, remote facilities, deployed headquarters, mine sites, communications sites and other constrained environments.

The result is powerful digital capability with a dramatically reduced physical footprint.

Compute where it’s needed. Data where it needs to remain.

Built to
operate disconnected.

Connectivity isn’t always guaranteed. In many of the environments we support, networks can be intermittent, bandwidth constrained, contested or completely unavailable.

Mallee C2 designs environments that can continue providing local compute, data, applications and AI capability without relying on continuous external connectivity.

When connectivity becomes available, architectures can be designed to reconnect, synchronise and exchange information with broader enterprise environments.

Connected when possible. Independent when necessary.
